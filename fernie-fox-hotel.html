Case Study · Web + SEO

Fernie Fox Hotel: from quiet homepage to a real booking driver.

A full website rebuild paired with a year of ongoing local SEO work, measured against the same month one year apart.

The challenge

Compete with bigger hotel marketing budgets, on a boutique hotel's resources.

Fernie Fox Hotel needed a website that could hold its own against OTA-backed hotel marketing and larger regional competitors, and a way to turn local search visibility into actual calls, direction requests, and direct bookings, not just passive impressions.

What we did

A full rebuild, plus a sustained local SEO campaign.

We rebuilt the site on SEO-ready architecture from the ground up, then ran an ongoing local SEO campaign targeting the searches Fernie visitors actually use, alongside a fully optimized Google Business Profile to capture high-intent local search traffic. The same technical foundation is also what's starting to earn the site referrals from AI assistants like ChatGPT.
